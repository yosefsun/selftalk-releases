# Selftalk

Gentle on-screen affirmations for your Mac, in your language and your voice (formal, casual, witty or meme).

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/yosefsun/selftalk-releases/releases/latest/download/Selftalk.zip)** · [Website](https://selftalk.pages.dev/)

This repository hosts the public downloads and the update feed (`appcast.xml`) for Selftalk. Free to try, with a 7-day Premium trial. Self-help reminder tool, not a medical device.
